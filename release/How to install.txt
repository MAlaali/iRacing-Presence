iRacing Discord Presence 1.1.0
==============================

Shows what you're doing in iRacing on your Discord profile:

    Playing iRacing
      GT Sprint · Race
      Porsche 911 GT3 R (992) @ Spa  (3 of 24)
      12:34 left

with a picture of your car and the track. It works on its own: it starts with Windows, sits
in the notification area, and updates your status whenever you're in a session.


INSTALL (one minute)
--------------------
1. Double-click  iRacingPresence-Setup.exe  and click Yes.

   Windows may show "Windows protected your PC", because this is a small hobby app that
   isn't signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

2. A message says it's running. Look for the "iR" icon near the clock (it may be hidden
   behind the ^ arrow).

3. In Discord, check that Settings > Activity Privacy > "Share my activity" is on.

That's it. Start iRacing and join a session.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Series names: iRacing only tells the app a series number, not its name. The first time you
  race in a new series, right-click the "iR" icon > "Name this series..." and type e.g.
  "GT Sprint". It remembers it from then on.
- Seeing two iRacing entries on your profile? The extra plain one is Discord's own game
  detection; remove iRacing under Discord Settings > Registered Games.
- Hide your status for a while: right-click the "iR" icon > "Hide my iRacing status".
- Update: run a newer iRacingPresence-Setup.exe the same way. Your settings are kept.
- Uninstall: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > iRacing Discord Presence > Uninstall.


WHICH FILE?
-----------
iRacingPresence-Setup.exe       ~48 MB   Works on any 64-bit Windows 10/11 PC. Use this one.
iRacingPresence-Setup-lite.exe  ~0.3 MB  Same app, small enough to send in a Discord chat, but it
                                          needs Microsoft's free ".NET 9 Desktop Runtime". If it's
                                          missing, Windows offers to download it on first launch.
